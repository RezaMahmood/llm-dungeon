# Signing In to LLM Dungeon Adventure

## How to sign in

1. Open the application.
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
3. Complete the Microsoft sign-in flow with your Microsoft account.
4. You'll land on the main menu, showing the options your account has access to.

## What the menu items mean

- **Start or Continue Game** — shown if your account has the Player role.
- **Administration** — shown if your account has the Administrator role.
- If neither appears, your account is registered but no role has been
  assigned yet — contact an administrator.

## If sign-in fails

- **"Sign in was cancelled"** — you closed the Microsoft sign-in window;
  click the button again to retry.
- **"Sign in failed"** — a network error occurred; check your connection and
  try again.
- **"Access Not Granted"** — your Microsoft account is not on the
  application's allow-list; contact an administrator.

## How to sign out

Click **Sign out** in the main menu. This clears your session and returns
you to the login screen.

## Support

Contact your administrator if you believe you should have access but are
being denied.
